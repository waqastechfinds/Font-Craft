What is Font Craft?

Font Craft is a free online collection of fancy font and Unicode text tools. It helps you turn ordinary text into eye-catching styles that you can copy and paste into social profiles, messages and creative projects.

No app installation, account or complicated setup is required. Enter your text, choose a style and copy your result instantly.

What you can do

Fancy Font ChangerGenerate aesthetic Unicode styles including bold, script, gothic, bubble and more.
Social Media TextCreate Instagram bios, TikTok text, WhatsApp statuses and Discord messages.
Creative TitlesDesign decorated headings, profile names and eye-catching text layouts.
Quick & AccessibleUse the tools on mobile, tablet or desktop without signing up.

Our mission

Our mission is to make text creativity simple and accessible for everyone. Font Craft is built for people who want to express themselves online without installing software or learning complicated design tools.

How it works

  • Type or paste your normal text into a Font Craft tool.
  • Select a style or formatting option.
  • Copy the result and paste it wherever you want.

About Waqas Tech Finds

Font Craft is created and maintained by Waqas Tech Finds, an independent technology brand focused on building useful, simple and accessible online tools.

We continue improving Font Craft with new styles, platform-specific tools, helpful guides and a better experience across devices.

Good to know

Font Craft creates Unicode characters rather than traditional font files. Because different apps and devices support Unicode differently, a style may appear slightly different—or not be supported—in some places.
