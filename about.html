About Font Craft

A simple, free and creative space for turning ordinary text into stylish Unicode fonts, bios, captions and titles.

What is Font Craft?

Font Craft is a browser-based collection of text tools made for creators, social-media users and anyone who wants their words to stand out.

Type your text, choose a style and copy the result. No installation or signup is required.

What you can create

  • Fancy and aesthetic Unicode text
  • Instagram bios and captions
  • WhatsApp status text
  • Discord names and messages
  • Decorated titles and creative layouts

Our goal

Our goal is to make creative text formatting quick, accessible and enjoyable on every device. Font Craft is designed to work directly in your browser, so you can create and copy stylish text wherever you need it.

Important note

These tools use Unicode characters rather than traditional font files. Their appearance can vary between apps and devices depending on platform support.
